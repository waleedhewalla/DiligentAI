Early – start with scope and metering

Open gaps

  • We know which of our exported products fall under CBAM (by CN code).
  • We have listed the EU customers and importers who will ask for our emissions data.
  • We know which installations and production routes make each product.
  • Fuel and energy use is metered per installation, not only per site.
  • Production quantities are recorded per product and per period.
  • We know our electricity consumption and its source.
  • We can get embedded-emissions data for key precursors from our suppliers.
  • System boundaries and production processes are defined for each installation.
  • Our monitoring and calculation method is documented, following the EU rules.
  • We can calculate direct and indirect embedded emissions per tonne of product.
  • Every reported figure traces back to meters, SCADA or ERP records.
  • We have a plan for verification by an accredited verifier.
  • We have a repeatable way to send emissions data to EU customers in the format they need.
