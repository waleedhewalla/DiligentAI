How Star Trans Eliminated Manual Planning in 8 Weeks

From a 3-day manual planning cycle in Excel to automated schedules, a live executive view and an Arabic content engine — all three Diligent AI products live in one company.

The challenge

Every week, the planning team spent two to three days building the schedule by hand in Excel. By the time it was published, reality had already moved: a vehicle was down, a shipment was late, a customer changed priority.

Leadership saw performance in a weekly PDF, days after the fact. Commercial teams relied on agencies for Arabic content, waiting weeks for each campaign.

Global planning suites were quoted at 12–18 months of implementation with English-only interfaces and no local support.

The solution

IPE — Operations Intelligence

IPE was connected to Star Trans' order and resource data and modelled their real constraints. The planning team now generates a full schedule in under a minute and re-plans on the spot when something changes.

CEO OS — Executive Intelligence

CEO OS brought operational KPIs from IPE together with finance and commercial data into a single Arabic executive view, replacing the Monday PDF.

Nexus AI — Commercial Intelligence

Nexus AI took over first drafts of Arabic LinkedIn content and proposals, with the commercial team reviewing instead of writing from scratch.

Results at 30 / 60 / 90 days

  1. Day 30

    Planning cycle cut from days to minutes. Planners shift their time from building schedules to managing exceptions.

  2. Day 60

    Leadership reviews live KPIs instead of weekly reports; exceptions are raised before the weekly meeting.

  3. Day 90

    80% reduction in planning time, with all three products in daily use across operations, leadership and commercial teams.
