Deploy proven AI tools — or build models on your own data.

Start from our pre-built tools for scheduling, executive intelligence and content, or have us build custom models for forecasting, quality and maintenance — Arabic-first and production-grade.

Typical duration: Typically 4–12 weeks

Discuss a build

Deliverables

  • Configured pre-built tool or custom model
  • Arabic-native user interface and reports
  • User training and hand-over
  • Measured baseline and 30/60/90-day review

How the engagement runs

  1. 1

    Scope

    Success metric and baseline agreed up front.

  2. 2

    Model

    Configure a pre-built tool or train on your history.

  3. 3

    Pilot

    Run beside the current process on one line or site.

  4. 4

    Scale

    Roll out, train users, measure against the baseline.

Integrate

Connect AI to the ERP, MES and legacy systems you already run.

AI is only as good as the data it sees and the systems it can act on. We connect SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, shop-floor systems and spreadsheets into reliable data pipelines — your ERP stays the system of record.

Typical duration: Typically 3–10 weeks

Plan an integration

Deliverables

  • ERP / MES connectors (read and write-back)
  • Governed data pipelines and a clean data layer
  • Monitoring, alerting and runbooks
  • Documentation your IT team can own

How the engagement runs

  1. 1

    Map

    Systems, interfaces, data owners and security constraints.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Standard connectors first; custom adapters where needed.

  3. 3

    Validate

    Reconcile against source systems before anything goes live.

  4. 4

    Operate

    Monitoring, SLAs and hand-over to your team.
