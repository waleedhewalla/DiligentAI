Pilot-to-Production Program

Across the Gulf, most companies use AI somewhere but few see financial returns. The gap is the step from proof-of-concept to production. This program is built around that step: a KPI agreed in writing, a fixed-scope pilot, a go/no-go on the numbers and a real hand-over.

Problems this solves

  • Pilots that impressed in a demo but never reached the floor
  • No agreed baseline, so no one can prove value
  • Models built outside the ERP that users ignore

What you get

Pilot health check

For stalled pilots: we review data, model and adoption and tell you what it takes to go live — or to stop.

KPI contract

Baseline, target and measurement method signed before work starts.

Built inside your systems

Results land in the ERP and the screens people already use.

Part of the fee can be tied to the agreed KPI

Pilot to production, in writing

Most AI pilots never reach the factory floor. Ours are scoped to go live: the success metric, the baseline and the go-live plan are agreed before we start.

Learn more — Pilot to production, in writing
  1. 1

    Baseline in writing

    We measure today's performance with you and sign off the KPI the pilot must move.

  2. 2

    Fixed-scope pilot

    One line or one site, a fixed timeline and a fixed price.

  3. 3

    Go / no-go on the numbers

    A joint review against the baseline decides the roll-out — not a slide deck.

  4. 4

    Production hand-over

    Integrated with your ERP/MES, users trained, monitored, with a 30/60/90-day review.
